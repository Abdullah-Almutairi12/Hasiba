---
name: Explicit inventory revaluation policy
description: Approval and correction boundaries for deliberate standard-cost revaluation
---
Standard-price edits remain prospective. Existing stock can be revalued only
through a separate, explicitly confirmed request approved by a financial actor
other than its creator. The proposed variance account is approved with that
request, not inferred as an automatic COGS adjustment.

**Why:** Editing a catalogue price is not authorization to change booked stock
or profit. Independent approval makes both the valuation and the chosen
income/expense offset an explicit financial decision.

**How to apply:** Preserve the separation when extending stock and accounting
flows. Corrections need another approved valuation based on current stock;
never reverse only its journal or rewrite historical physical movements.
The value-only ledger must be included alongside physical movements when
reconciling movement value to quants and Inventory.
