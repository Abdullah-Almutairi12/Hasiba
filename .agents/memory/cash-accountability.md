---
name: Cash accountability
description: Difference recording, financial review and responsibility decisions are separate business stages
---
Recording a till difference, reviewing its accounting treatment, and deciding responsibility are separate operations. A responsibility decision alone must never create employee debt or a payroll deduction.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires documented reasons, evidence, decision-maker and history without automatically charging employees.

**How to apply:** Retain the original closing and accounting evidence, bind any responsibility decision to those facts, and prevent retry duplication. Any later financial recovery or payroll action needs its own authorized process; do not infer it from a responsibility label.
